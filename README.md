📊 Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview :- 

The Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, regional trends, and business profitability. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations and KPIs, helping stakeholders make informed business decisions.

🚀 Key Features :-

💰 Total Sales Analysis

📦 Total Quantity Sold

🏷️ Discount Analysis

🚚 Average Delivery Days

🌍 Profit by City (Interactive Map)

📍 State-wise Sales Analysis

🌎 Region-wise Sales Performance

📂 Category-wise Sales Distribution

👥 Segment-wise Sales Analysis

🏆 Top 5 Best-Selling Products

📉 Bottom 5 Performing Products

🎛️ Dynamic Filters (Customer ID, Customer Name, Month, Year)



📊 Dashboard KPIs :- 

1) Total Sales : 2.3M

2) Total Quantity : 39K

3) Total Discount : 10K

4) Average Delivery Days : 4
   


📈 Visualizations :-

1) KPI Cards

2) Sales by State

3) Sales by Region

4) Profit by City (Map)

5) Top 5 Products

6) Bottom 5 Products

7) Category-wise Sales (Pie Chart)

8) Segment-wise Sales (Pie Chart)

9) Interactive Slicers
    


🛠️ Technologies Used :-

1) Microsoft Power BI

2) Power Query

3) DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

4) Data Modeling

5) Excel Dataset
   


🎯 Business Objectives :-


1) Monitor overall sales performance.

2) Analyze regional and state-wise sales trends.

3) Identify top-performing and low-performing products.

4) Evaluate customer purchasing patterns.

5) Measure category and segment performance.

6) Analyze profit distribution across cities.

7) Support business decisions using interactive dashboards.



💡 Key Insights :-


1) The West Region generated the highest sales.

2) Technology contributed the largest share of sales.

3) Consumer was the highest-performing customer segment.

4) The dashboard highlights the Top 5 and Bottom 5 products based on sales.

5) Interactive filters allow analysis by customer, month, and year.
